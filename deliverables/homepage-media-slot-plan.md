# Qingzheng homepage media and buyer-path plan

## Business interpretation

Qingzheng provides project-oriented automotive automation equipment, documented through eight customer project-image groups. The materials support fixtures, turntables, standard workstations, production/assembly-line projects and an engineering/manufacturing role. Buyer roles are treated as hypotheses: automotive OEM/Tier project engineers, manufacturing engineering and procurement teams. Target markets, certifications, cycle times, capacities, MOQ, lead time and commercial terms are not supplied and will not be invented.
